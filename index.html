Quiz and H5P Converter

Turns the CSV from the course agents into a Canvas quiz (.zip), or an H5P Question Set, Interactive Book, Course Presentation or Branching Scenario (.h5p), with all feedback included. The page works out which from the header row. Images named in the CSV can be added too. Everything happens in this page; nothing is uploaded anywhere.

2. Paste the CSV

Paste the whole CSV block from the agent, including the header row, or open a saved .csv file. Quiz CSVs start Pool,Pick,Type. Interactive Book and Course Presentation CSVs start Type,Title,Text (CHAPTER rows make a book; SLIDE rows make a presentation). Branching Scenario CSVs start ID,Type,Title,Text,Next. If the agent exported in batches, paste each batch below the last; repeated header rows are ignored.

Formatting inside Text: || starts a new paragraph, - starts a bullet, **bold**, ## starts a heading, and --- adds a separator line. Interactive Books also take ACCORDION rows (collapsible panels), CARDS rows (Dialog Cards that students turn over) and TABLE rows (rows split by ||, cells by |, first row as headings). LABEL rows make a Drag and Drop: students drag labels onto a picture; you click where each label belongs with Place labels. FILL rows make Fill in the Blanks: students type the words marked with asterisks, for example The *axon* carries the impulse, with other accepted answers after a slash (*neurone/neuron*). PART rows (from workbook conversions) start a chapter: the pages after it become sections of that chapter, each with its title as a heading.

Images (optional)

If the CSV has an Image column, add those image files here (PNG, JPG, GIF or SVG). They are matched by file name and placed in the Interactive Book, Course Presentation or Branching Scenario. Quizzes ignore images.

Slides from a lecture: when the CSV names a slide, such as Pain_mechanisms.pdf#12, add that lecture saved as a PDF here and the page turns slide 12 into an image. Once a PDF is added, you can also choose slides yourself: each page, text block or card in the preview gets a Choose a slide button. Click any slide image to crop it to the figure. Save PowerPoint files as PDF first, keeping the same name. The PDF stays on your computer.

Pictures from a Word document: when the CSV names a figure or question, such as Workbook.docx#Figure 2 or Workbook.docx#Q8, add that Word file (.docx) here. The page takes out its pictures and matches them to the figure captions and questions. Check each one in the preview, and use Change picture to pick a different one. Pictures that sit side by side in Word are kept side by side, and pictures keep the size they have in the Word file. Labels drawn in Word on top of a picture are not part of the picture, so they do not come across.

Only use images you have the right to share: check figures taken from textbooks or other publishers before they go into FLO, and never use images of real patients without consent.

3. Download

Paste a CSV to see a summary.

Canvas New Quiz

Then in Canvas: create a New Quiz with the same title, open Build, select the Options menu (three dots) > Import Content, and upload the .zip. Leave it unpublished while you preview it. Set attempts, time limit, dates and when students see feedback in the quiz Settings.

H5P Question Set (practice)

Then in H5P: choose Add content > Upload, select the .h5p file, then check it in preview before adding it to Canvas.

H5P Interactive Book

Used for the book's header, buttons and progress bar.

No cover image. The cover shows the title and description only.

Then in H5P: Add content > Upload, then preview every chapter before adding it to Canvas.

H5P Course Presentation

Each slide has a title, then its text and one activity. Then in H5P: Add content > Upload, then check every slide in preview. To move or resize anything on a slide, open it in the H5P editor.

H5P Branching Scenario

Then in H5P: Add content > Upload, then play through every route before adding it to Canvas.
